Privacy Policy for RandList

Last updated: 2026-10-30

RandList is an iOS and macOS application designed with privacy in mind. Random generation and the app’s core functionality are performed locally on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

RandList stores app settings, generation history, favorites, and reusable templates locally on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. This information is used only to provide the app’s features.

iCloud Synchronization

RandList can use Apple iCloud to synchronize generation history and favorite templates between devices signed in to the same iCloud account. History and Favorites synchronization can be controlled independently in the app’s Settings.

When synchronization is enabled, the corresponding information is transmitted to and stored in your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. This may include generated results, generation settings, custom list items, favorites, reusable templates, record identifiers, modification information, and deletion status required to keep your devices synchronized.

RandList does not use iCloud data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking. RandList does not receive your Apple ID password, payment information, contacts, photos, location, or other unrelated iCloud data. Apple processes iCloud data in accordance with Apple’s own privacy policy and iCloud terms.

Synchronization Controls

  • History and Favorites synchronization can be enabled or disabled separately.
  • Both synchronization options are enabled by default.
  • RandList remains fully functional when synchronization is disabled or unavailable.
  • Disabling synchronization does not delete data already stored locally or in iCloud.
  • Changes made while offline may be merged when synchronization resumes.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual history records and favorites, delete an entire day of history, or clear all applicable records using the controls provided in RandList. When iCloud synchronization is enabled, these deletion actions are synchronized to your other devices.

Deleted content is removed from the active History or Favorites collection. Limited deletion metadata may be retained as necessary to communicate the deletion to other synchronized devices and prevent deleted content from reappearing.

You may also manage or remove data stored in iCloud through the iCloud storage controls provided by Apple on your device. Removing RandList from a device does not necessarily delete data already stored in iCloud.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

RandList does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising frameworks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Data brokers
  • Third-party data collection SDKs

RandList does not sell user data or share synchronized content with advertisers or data brokers.

Network Access

RandList accesses the network only when necessary to provide iCloud synchronization and related Apple CloudKit functionality. The app does not operate its own user-account server and does not require a separate RandList account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RandList’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
