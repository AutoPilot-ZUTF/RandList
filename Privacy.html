Privacy Policy for RandList

Last updated: 2026-05-08

RandList is an iOS application designed with privacy in mind.

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All user data, including input history and app settings, is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad and never leaves your device.

RandList does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising frameworks
  • Tracking technologies
  • Third-party data collection

No data is sent to external servers or shared with third parties.
